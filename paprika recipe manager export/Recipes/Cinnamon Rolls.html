Cinnamon Rolls

Dessert

Source: Github.com

Ingredients

Dough

1 Tbsp Dry yeast (one 1/4 packet = 2.25 teaspoons)

1 Cup Warm milk (temperature that's specified on yeast packet)

1/3 Cup Sugar

1/2 Cup Soft Butter

1 tsp Salt

2 Eggs

4 Cup Flour


To Spread:

1/4 Melted Cup Butter (OG recipe says softened, but I've always done melted)

Filling

1 Cup Brown Sugar

3 Tbsp Cinnamon


Frosting

1/2 Cup Soft Butter

3/2 Cup Powdered Sugar

1 oz of Cream Cheese (doubling this works)

2 Tbsp Whipping Cream

1 tsp Vanilla Extract

Pinch of Salt

Directions

Part 1: Dough:

Dissolve yeast in warm milk

Start preheating oven to ~200 °F

Add the rest of the ingredients and mix well.

Knead with your KitchenAid

Take mixing bowl, cover with towel and place in the oven until double in size (about an hour)

Part 2: filling & Baking
Start preheating oven to 400 °F

Roll out into a square that is roughly 1/4 inch thick

Spread Butter over dough

Mix brown sugar and cinnamon

Spread the mixture evenly over the dough

Tightly roll dough

Cut dough in half if needed

Cut roll into 1 inch segments and place in a greased metal pan

Let rise until double in size

Put in oven for 10 minutes

Part 3: Frosting

Mix all ingredients together with your KitchenAid, beat until fluffy.
