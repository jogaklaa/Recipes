Mango Chicken Salad

Source: Github.com

Ingredients

Salad

1 Head Romaine Letuce

3 Tomatoes, Diced

1/2 Cup Feta Cheese

1 Mango Diced

2 Chicken Breasts (cooked and seasoned with lemon pepper)

1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds

1 Bag Tortilla Chips

2 Cups Warm Cooked Rice (Jasmine is choice)


Dressing

1/4 Cup Dijon Mustard

2 Tbsp Lime Juice

1 tsp Chili Powder

3/4 Cups Mayonnaise

1/2 Cup White Vinegar

1/4 Cup Soy Sauce

1/2 Cup White Sugar

2 Tbsp Minced Garlic (garlic powder also works)

2 Tbsp Ginger Powder

Directions

Mix Dressing ingredients together until smooth

TOSS THAT SALAD
