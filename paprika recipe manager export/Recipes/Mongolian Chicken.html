Mongolian Chicken

Source: Github.com

Ingredients

1 Lb Chicken Breast or Thighs Cut into Thin Strips

1 Tbsp Cornstarch

2 Tbsp Oil (one with a high smoking point)

2 tsp Fresh Ginger, Minced

1 Tbsp Garlic Cloves, Minced

2 Bunches of Green Onions ( cut into 2 inch pieces, separating white and green parts)


Sauce

2 Tbsp Hoisin Sauce

1 tsp Sesame Oil

1/4 tsp Black Pepper

1/2 Cup Light Soy Sauce (tastes too salty with regular)

1/2 Cup Water

2 tsp Cornstarch

1/2 Cup Brown Sugar

Directions

Combine sauce ingredients in a bowl, whisking until cornstarch and brown sugar is dissolved, set bowl aside

Toss the Chicken in cornstarch to coat

Heat wok or heavy frying pan over high heat, once hot add oil

Add Chicken and cook through (original recipe says about a minute)

Add in Garlic and Ginger and fry for another 30 seconds

Add Sauce and white parts of the green onions, simmer until thickened ("about a minute")

Add in the remaining green onion parts and stir to coat.
