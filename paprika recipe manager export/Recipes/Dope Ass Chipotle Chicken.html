Dope Ass Chipotle Chicken

Source: Github.com

Ingredients

1/2 Medium Red Onion Chopped

3 Cloves of Garlic Chopped

2 Tbsp Adobo Sauce (from can of chipotle peppers in adobo sauce)

2 Tbsp Olive Oil

2 Tbsp Ancho Chile Powder

1 tsp Ground Cumin

1 tsp Dried Oregano

1 Tbsp Salt

1/2 tsp Black Pepper

Water (as needed)

3-4 lb Boneless, Skinless Chicken Thighs

Directions

Marinade

Place onion, garlic, adobo sauce, olive oil, chile powder, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper into a food processor (blender if you must) and process until smooth

Pour marinade into a measuring cup and add water until 1 cup total is reached

Using a fork, poke holes all over the chicken and place in a resealable bag.

Pour marinade over, seal and move the marinade to cover the chicken evenly

Refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight

Cooking

Preheat a skillet over medium heat and add some olive oil

Place chicken into pan to cook, turning the chicken occasionally until cooked through

I recommend cooking in two batches and adding more oil in between, if needed

Remove the chicken to a large cutting board and allow to rest for 10 minutes

slice or chop the chicken, as desired and serve
