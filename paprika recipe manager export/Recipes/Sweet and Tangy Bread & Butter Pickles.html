Sweet and Tangy Bread & Butter Pickles

Source: Github.com

Ingredients

2 lbs Pickling Cucumbers

2.5 Cups Sugar

2.5 Cups White Vinegar

1 tsp Salt


Spice Mix:

2 tsp Minced Onions

1 tsp Celery Seed

1 tsp Mustard Seed

3/4 Whole Allspice

1/2 tsp Ground Turmeric


Non-Food Stuff

Two 32 oz jars

Two lids and bands

Directions

Cut ends of cucumbers

Cut rest of cucumbers into 1/2 inch slices and place in glass bowl

Mix ingredients under spice mix, sugar, vinegar and salt in a large sauce pan

Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, making sure the sugar dissolves

Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes

Refrigerator Pickles

Pour hot liquid over cucumbers in bowl, making sure that all surfaces of the cucumbers have been touched

Let cool to room temperature (takes at least an hour)

Pack pickles evenly into jars

Ladle pickling liquid into the jars

Add lids/bands and place in fridge

Best after 3 weeks, eat within 3 months
