Bourbon Chicken

Chicken, Mains

Source: Github.com

Ingredients

Part 1:

1 Tbsp Olive Oil

2 lb Chicken Breasts (bite-size pieces)


Part 2:

1 Clove of Garlic (crushed)

1/4 tsp of Ginger (dried or fresh minced)

3/4 tsp Red Pepper Flakes

1/4 cup Apple Juice

1/3 cup Brown Sugar

2 Tbsp Ketchup

1 Tbsp Cider Vinegar

1/2 cup Water

1 Tbsp Cornstarch (cause we like it thicc)

Directions

Heat pan/wok, add oil followed by the chicken. Cook until lightly browned

Blend ingredients from part 2 in a separate bowl and add to pan

Heat to a hard boil

Reduce heat and let simmer until the sauce is adequate reduced

Serve over a dope amount of rice
