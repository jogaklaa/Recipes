Pizza Sauce

Source: Github.com

Ingredients

1 28-Ounce Can Crushed Tomatoes

2 Medium to Large Garlic Cloves (Minced, grated or garlic pressed)

2 Tbsp Olive Oil

1.5 tsp Salt

Directions

Add Tomatoes, garlic and oil into a pan

Warm pan up to a medium-high heat to get warmed up

Once it starts to bubble, reduce heat to a low simmer and leave uncovered for 10 to 15 minutes or until liquid is reduced

Store leftovers for up to a week
